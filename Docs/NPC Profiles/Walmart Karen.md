# Walmart Karen

## Role

`Walmart Karen` is the first strong solo opponent archetype for `Pain Olympics`.

She should feel like:
- public entitlement in human form
- racist grievance-performance
- anti-empathy, pro-humiliation
- permanently convinced she is the real victim
- loud, theatrical, and weirdly energized by minor inconvenience

## Writing Angle

The joke should be about behavior, not just class markers.

Good targets:
- fake victim complex
- manager-summoning instincts
- coupon warfare
- Facebook conspiracy brain
- casual xenophobia and racism
- treating boundaries or policies like civil-rights violations
- humiliating workers in public and calling it honesty

Avoid making the whole character just:
- `poor white trash`
- incest jokes as the main punchline
- generic poverty ridicule

That material can show up at the edges, but it should not be the whole engine. The sharper version of the character is someone who turns every room into a hostage situation because she thinks inconvenience is oppression.

## Tone Rules

- No slurs in card text or dialogue.
- Satirize cruelty, not vulnerability.
- Keep the voice blunt, memeable, and quotable.
- She should sound convinced, not self-aware.
- Her humor comes from inflated self-importance and persecution fantasy.

## Visual Notes

- Current placeholder portrait: meme-style image
- Good props later: dashboard selfie angle, cigarette, oversized sunglasses, live-stream posture, aggressive nameplate treatment
- Board presence should feel like a contestant who thinks the show exists to validate her

## Deck Identity

`Walmart Karen` should play like:
- grievance as strategy
- paranoia as self-justification
- culture-war fixation
- humiliation of workers and strangers
- civic decay filtered through Facebook aunt energy

## Dialogue Flavor

She should talk like:
- she is filing a complaint in real time
- every sentence is an accusation
- the room owes her an apology before anything has happened

- "I'm not upset, I'm documenting this."
- "Interesting. So we're punishing honesty now."
